# EstateHub: The Modern Solution for Estate Management

## Product Manual & Feature Overview

---

### 1. Introduction: Simplify Your Estate Management

Welcome to **EstateHub**, the all-in-one application designed to modernize and streamline the management of residential and commercial estates. In today's fast-paced world, managing finances, personnel, and resident services can be complex and time-consuming. EstateHub replaces cumbersome spreadsheets and outdated software with a single, intuitive, and powerful platform.

Our application empowers administrators with robust financial tools, enhances security with a modern QR-based pass system, and provides residents and staff with a transparent and easy-to-use portal for all their needs.

**Why choose EstateHub?**
*   **Centralized Control**: Manage all aspects of your estate from a single, powerful dashboard.
*   **Financial Transparency**: Automate maintenance calculations, fine applications, and view detailed financial ledgers with our integrated cashbook.
*   **Enhanced Security**: Implement a secure and efficient QR code-based system for resident, vendor, and staff access.
*   **Role-Based Access**: Provide tailored portals for Admins, Apartment Owners, Contractors, and Security personnel, ensuring everyone has the tools they need without unnecessary complexity.
*   **Fully Customizable**: From application branding to individual page layouts, EstateHub adapts to your society's unique requirements.

---

### 2. Key Features at a Glance

*   **Comprehensive Admin Dashboard**: At-a-glance KPIs for financial health, user management, and pending actions.
*   **Automated Financials**: Sophisticated maintenance calculation based on apartment size, with automatic late fees and daily fines.
*   **Integrated Cashbook**: A complete, double-entry style cashbook for a full financial overview of receipts and payments.
*   **QR Code Pass System**: Secure, scannable QR codes for every user, verifiable through the Security portal.
*   **Customizable User Portals**: Each role gets a unique dashboard and set of tools. Admins can customize the layout of any page for any role.
*   **Entity Enrollment & Management**: Easily enroll new residents, staff, and contractors, either manually or via CSV import.
*   **Receipt Verification Workflow**: Residents can make payments, and Admins can verify and log them in the system.

### 3. User Portals: A Tailored Experience for Everyone

EstateHub provides four distinct portals, each designed for a specific role.

#### A. The Admin Portal: Your Command Center

The Admin portal offers complete control over the entire estate.

*   **Dashboard**: The first thing you see is a powerful dashboard with Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). Track apartments with dues, pending payment verifications, and active staff at a glance.
    `[Screenshot of the Admin Dashboard showing KPI cards]`
*   **Cashbook**: A professional-grade cashbook provides a detailed ledger of all financial transactions, separating receipts and payments for ultimate clarity on your society's cash flow.
    `[Screenshot of the Cashbook page with transaction rows]`
*   **Receipts & Payments**: View a complete history of all payments, filter for pending verifications, and mark payments as 'Paid' to complete the transaction. Create new receipts for any user in the system.
    `[Screenshot of the Receipts page showing a pending verification]`
*   **Enrollment**: Add new users to the system one by one through an intuitive form or import hundreds of users at once with a simple CSV file.
*   **Settings**: A dedicated settings area allows you to configure every aspect of the application:
    *   **Global Settings**: Customize the society name, logo, and login page branding.
    *   **Admin Settings**: Manage financial rates for maintenance, contractor passes, and late fees.
    *   **Accounts**: Create and manage financial accounts, set depreciation rates, and define calculation start dates for arrears.
    *   **Personnel Settings**: Manage work shifts for security staff.
    `[Screenshot of the Admin Settings page showing rate management cards]`
*   **Layout Customization**: Uniquely, Admins can visually design the page layouts for *any* user role. Drag and drop KPI cards to create the perfect dashboard or utility page for your residents, security, or contractors.
    `[Screenshot of the 'Customize' page showing the drag-and-drop interface]`

#### B. The Apartment Owner Portal: Clarity and Convenience

Residents get a clean, simple portal to manage their finances and access.

*   **Personal Dashboard**: A welcoming dashboard shows the resident's profile, their personal QR code pass, and a summary of their maintenance status.
*   **Charges & Payment History**: This is more than just a payment list. It's a detailed, itemized ledger showing exactly how their balance is calculated. It includes monthly maintenance charges, any late fees or daily fines applied, and all payments made. The running balance makes it easy to see dues owed or credit available.
    `[Screenshot of the 'Charges & Payment History' card with debits, credits, and a running balance]`
*   **Personal Settings**: Residents can update their contact information and change their password.

#### C. The Security Portal: Efficient and Secure

The Security portal is built for speed and efficiency, focusing on core security tasks.

*   **QR Code Scanner**: The primary tool is a large, easy-to-use QR code scanner. Security staff can point their device's camera at a user's QR code to instantly evaluate their pass status (PASS/FAIL).
    `[Screenshot of the Scan Card UI with the camera view and a 'PASS' verdict]`
*   **Attendance Relay**: Simple "Clock In" and "Clock Out" buttons for logging shifts.
*   **Personal Pass**: Security staff also have their own QR code for identification.

#### D. The Contractor Portal: Simple Access Management

Vendors and contractors have a straightforward portal to manage their access passes.

*   **Dashboard**: Shows their service type and a QR code for their current vendor pass.
*   **Payment History**: A log of all pass purchase payments.
